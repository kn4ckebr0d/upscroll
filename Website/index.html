How to play

Scroll down to go up

You already know the move. It's the pull you do a hundred times a day, turned into a climb.

1

Pull the world down

Put a finger on the wall and drag down. The wall comes with you, and up you go.

2

Hand over hand

Running out of screen? Put your other finger down and keep pulling, just like a real climber.

3

Don't let go

Slip and you slide back to the last checkpoint. Swipe sideways to dodge trouble, grab coins, and race to the rocket.

4

Blast off

Every rocket takes you to a new planet, with its own colors and its own twist.
